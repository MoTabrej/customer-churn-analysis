# Customer Churn Analysis

Customer Churn Analysis project using Python, SQL and Data Analytics to identify customer churn patterns, high-risk customer segments and revenue impact.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer subscription data to understand:

- Overall customer churn rate
- Monthly vs annual contract churn
- Revenue impact of customer churn
- Customer lifetime value (CLTV)
- High-risk customer segments
- Support and cancellation patterns
- Factors associated with customer churn

## Key Findings

- Overall churn rate: **28.6%**
- Monthly-contract customers showed a **55.6% churn rate**
- Annual-contract customers showed an **8.3% churn rate**
- Monthly-contract churn was approximately **6.7× higher** than annual-contract churn
- Estimated MRR leakage: **$73.94**
- Estimated CLTV erosion: **$2,047**

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize retention efforts for high-risk monthly subscribers.
- Encourage migration from monthly to annual contracts.
- Focus retention strategies on high-value customers.
- Use churn-risk segmentation to prioritize customer outreach.
- Investigate support interactions and cancellation drivers.
